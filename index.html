How to play

Steer: drag left/right, or ←/→ and A/D. Let go and the wheel straightens up.
Nitro: hold two fingers on the screen (steer with the first), or hold ↑, W, Space or Shift. Faster, and every metre on nitro scores double, but the steering gets twitchy. A full tank lasts 5 seconds; grab the blue nitro bottles on the road to top it up.
No brakes. The car speeds up on its own and keeps getting faster the further you go. Pressing ↓ won't help.
Damage: every hit costs health, more the faster and harder it is. Clipping a car you're overtaking or a barrel hurts a little; a concrete barrier head-on at full speed is the end. When the meter runs out the car is totaled. Grab the wrenches on the road to patch it up.
Bridge out: hit a ramp in front of a collapsed bridge to jump it. Miss the ramp and you're in the river.
Oil makes you slide. Turbo pads fire you forward. Cones are fair game.
Score: 1 per metre (2 on nitro), 25 per close call, 50 for flying over a car, 100 per bridge jumped, plus an airtime bonus.
Sound: M or the speaker button to mute.
